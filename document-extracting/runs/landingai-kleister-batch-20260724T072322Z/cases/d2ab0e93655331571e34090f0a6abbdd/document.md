You hereby agree to notify the Company immediately upon discovery of any unauthorized use or disclosure of Evaluation Material or any other breach of this letter agreement by you or any of your Representatives, and will cooperate with the Company to assist the Company to regain possession of the Evaluation Material and prevent its further unauthorized use or disclosure.

3. **Non-Solicitation; Non-Hire.**
