# Projection: Using Inner Products (I)

  Projection of $\mathbf{x}$ along the direction $\mathbf{a}$ ($\| \mathbf{a}\| = 1$).
      $$\mathbf{p} = \mathbf{a} \left(\mathbf{a} ^ {\mathrm{T}} \mathbf{x}\right)$$
    $$\left\| \mathbf{a} \right\| = \mathbf{a} ^ {\mathrm{T}} \mathbf{a} = 1$$
