where the decomposition follows by the chain rule of KL divergence.

    Next, we derive the model likelihood $\log \mu^{\theta}$. Notice that the marginal density $p_t^\theta(x)$ obeys the Fokker Plank equation
          $$\frac{\partial p _ {t} ^ {\theta} (x)}{\partial t} = - \nabla \cdot \left(p _ {t} ^ {\theta} (x) \sigma Z _ {t} ^ {\theta} (x)\right) + \frac{\sigma^ {2}}{2} \Delta p _ {t} ^ {\theta} (x) \tag{30}$$

where the last equality follows by Equation 15. Now, invoke Itô formula

      $$\begin{array}{l} \mathrm{d} \log p _ {t} ^ {\theta} = \frac{\partial \log p _ {t} ^ {\theta}}{\partial t} \mathrm{d} t + \left[ \nabla \log p _ {t} ^ {\theta} \cdot \sigma Z _ {t} ^ {\theta} + \frac{\sigma^ {2}}{2} \Delta \log p _ {t} ^ {\theta} \right] \mathrm{d} t + \sigma \nabla \log p _ {t} ^ {\theta} \cdot \mathrm{d} W _ {t} \\ = \left[ - \nabla \cdot \left(\sigma Z _ {t} ^ {\theta}\right) + \sigma^ {2} \Delta \log p _ {t} ^ {\theta} + \frac{1}{2} \| \sigma \nabla \log p _ {t} ^ {\theta} \| ^ {2} \right] \mathrm{d} t + \sigma \nabla \log p _ {t} ^ {\theta} \cdot \mathrm{d} W _ {t}, \tag{32} \\ \end{array}$$
where the last equality follows by Equation 31. Equation 32 facilitates a computation of the parametrized model likelihood

  $$\begin{array}{l} \log \mu^ {\theta} (X _ {0}) = \mathbb{E} _ {p _ {1} ^ {\theta}} [ \log p _ {1} ^ {\theta} (X _ {1} ^ {\theta}) ] - \int_ {0} ^ {1} \mathbb{E} _ {p _ {t} ^ {\theta}} \left[ \mathrm{d} \log p _ {t} ^ {\theta} \right] \\ = \mathbb{E} _ {p _ {1} ^ {\theta}} [ \log p _ {1} ^ {\theta} (X _ {1} ^ {\theta}) ] - \int_ {0} ^ {1} \mathbb{E} _ {p _ {t} ^ {\theta}} \left[ - \nabla \cdot (\sigma Z _ {t} ^ {\theta}) + \sigma^ {2} \Delta \log p _ {t} ^ {\theta} + \frac{1}{2} \| \sigma \nabla \log p _ {t} ^ {\theta} \| ^ {2} \right] \mathrm{d} t \\ = \mathbb{E} _ {p _ {1} ^ {\theta}} \left[ \log p _ {1} ^ {\theta} \left(X _ {1} ^ {\theta}\right) \right] - \int_ {0} ^ {1} \mathbb{E} _ {p _ {t} ^ {\theta}} \left[ - \nabla \cdot \left(\sigma Z _ {t} ^ {\theta}\right) - \frac{1}{2} \| \sigma \nabla \log p _ {t} ^ {\theta} \| ^ {2} \right] d t, \tag{33} \\ \end{array}$$
