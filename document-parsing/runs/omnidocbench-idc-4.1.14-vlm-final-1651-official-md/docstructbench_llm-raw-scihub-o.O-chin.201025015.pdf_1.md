# Mixed Cations and Structural Complexity in $(\mathrm{Eu}_{1 - x}\mathrm{Ca}_x)_4\mathrm{In}_3\mathrm{Ge}_4$ and $(\mathrm{Eu}_{1 - x}\mathrm{Ca}_x)_3\mathrm{In}_2\mathrm{Ge}_3$ — The First Two Members of the Homologous Series

$\mathbf{A}_{2[n + m]}\mathrm{In}_{2n + m}\mathbf{Ge}_{2[n + m]}$ ($\mathbf{n}, \mathbf{m} = 1, 2, \dots \infty ; \mathbf{A}: \text{Ca, Sr, Ba, Eu, or Yb}$). — $\mathrm{Eu_{1.23}Ca_{2.77}In_3Ge_4}$ (I), $\mathrm{Eu}_{2.60}\mathrm{Ca}_{1.40}\mathrm{In}_3\mathrm{Ge}_4$ (II), $\mathrm{Eu}_{0.30}\mathrm{Ca}_{2.70}\mathrm{In}_2\mathrm{Ge}_3$ (III), and $\mathrm{Eu}_{0.66}\mathrm{Ca}_{2.34}\mathrm{In}_2\mathrm{Ge}_3$ (IV) are synthesized from the elements in a molten In metal flux ($960^{\circ}\mathrm{C}, 20\mathrm{h}$). The compounds are characterized by single crystal XRD, magnetic susceptibility measurements, and TB-LMTO band structure calculations. (I) and (II) crystallize in the space group C2/m with $Z = 2$, and (III) and (IV) in the space group Pnma with $Z = 4$. The polyanionic substructures of the compounds are based on $\mathrm{InGe_4}$ edge-sharing tetrahedra, $\mathrm{Ge}_2$ dimers, and bridging In atoms in a nearly square planar environment. — (YOU, T.-S.; TOBASH, P.H.; BOBEV*, S.; Inorg. Chem. 49 (2010) 4, 1773-1783; Dep. Chem. Biochem., Univ. Del., Newark, DE 19716, USA; Eng.) W. Pewestorf
