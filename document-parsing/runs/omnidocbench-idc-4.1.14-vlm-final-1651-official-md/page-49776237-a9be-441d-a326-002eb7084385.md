# 第二章 行列式

    $$\left| \begin{array}{\text{ccccc} c} {\binom{r + 1} {r}} & {\binom{r} {r}} & 0 & \dots & 0 & 0 \\ {\binom{r + 1} {r - 1}} & {\binom{r + 1} {r}} & {\binom{r + 1} {r + 1}} & \dots & 0 & 0 \\ \vdots & \vdots & \vdots & & \vdots & \vdots \\ {\binom{n - 3} {r - 1}} & {\binom{n - 3} {r}} & {\binom{n - 3} {r + 1}} & \dots & {\binom{n - 3} {n - 3}} & 0 \\ {\binom{n - 2} {r - 1}} & {\binom{n - 2} {r}} & {\binom{n - 2} {r + 1}} & \dots & {\binom{n - 2} {n - 3}} & {\binom{n - 2} {n - 2}} \\ {\binom{n - 1} {r - 1}} & {\binom{n - 1} {r}} & {\binom{n - 1} {r + 1}} & \dots & {\binom{n - 1} {n - 3}} & {\binom{n - 1} {n - 2}} \end{array} \right| =$$
$$\begin{array}{l} \left| \begin{array}{\text{ccccc} c} \binom{r} {r - 1} & \binom{r} {r} & 0 & \dots & 0 & 0 \\ \binom{r + 1} {r - 1} & \binom{r + 1} {r} & \binom{r + 1} {r + 1} & \dots & 0 & 0 \\ \vdots & \vdots & \vdots & & \vdots & \vdots \\ \binom{n - 3} {r - 1} & \binom{n - 3} {r} & \binom{n - 3} {r + 1} & \dots & \binom{n - 3} {n - 3} & 0 \\ \binom{n - 2} {r - 1} & \binom{n - 2} {r} & \binom{n - 2} {r + 1} & \dots & \binom{n - 2} {n - 3} & \binom{n - 2} {n - 2} \\ \binom{n - 1} {r - 1} & \binom{n - 1} {r} & \binom{n - 1} {r + 1} & \dots & \binom{n - 1} {n - 3} & \binom{n - 1} {n - 2} \end{array} \right| + \left| \begin{array}{\text{ccccc} c} \binom{r} {r} & 0 & 0 & \dots & 0 & 0 \\ \binom{r + 1} {r - 1} & \binom{r + 1} {r} & \binom{r + 1} {r + 1} & \dots & 0 & 0 \\ \vdots & \vdots & \vdots & & \vdots & \vdots \\ \binom{n - 3} {r} & \binom{n - 3} {r - 1} & \binom{n - 3} {r - 1} & \dots & \binom{n - 3} {n - 3} & 0 \\ \binom{n - 2} {r - 1} & \binom{n - 2} {r - 1} & \binom{n - 2} {r} & \dots & \binom{n - 2} {n - 3} & \binom{n - 2} {n - 2} \\ \binom{n - 1} {r - 1} & \binom{n - 1} {r} & \binom{n - 1} {r + 1} & \dots & \binom{n - 1} {n - 3}. \end{array} \right| \\ = D (n - 1, r - 1) + D (n - 1, r). \\ \end{array}$$
因为 $D(n,n-1) = \left|\binom{n}{n-1}\right| = \binom{n}{n-1}$，

$D(n,0) = \begin{vmatrix} 1 & \binom{1}{1} & 0 & \dots & 0 & 0\\ 1 & \binom{2}{1} & \binom{2}{2} & \dots & 0 & 0\\ \vdots & \vdots & \vdots & & \vdots & \vdots \\ 1 & \binom{n-2}{1} & \binom{n-2}{2} & \dots & \binom{n-2}{n-2} & 0\\ 1 & \binom{n-1}{1} & \binom{n-1}{2} & \dots & \binom{n-1}{n-2} & \binom{n-1}{n-1}\\ 1 & \binom{n}{1} & \binom{n}{2} & \dots & \binom{n}{n-2} & \binom{n}{n-1} \end{vmatrix}$
  $$= \left| \begin{array}{\text{lllll} l} 1 & 1 & 0 & \dots & 0 & 0 \\ 0 & 1 & \binom{1} {1} & \dots & 0 & 0 \\ \vdots & \vdots & \vdots & & \vdots & \vdots \\ 0 & 1 & \binom{n - 3} {1} & \dots & \binom{n - 3} {n - 3} & 0 \\ 0 & 1 & \binom{n - 2} {1} & \dots & \binom{n - 2} {n - 3} & \binom{n - 2} {n - 2} \\ 0 & 1 & \binom{n - 1} {1} & \dots & \binom{n - 1} {n - 3} & \binom{n - 1} {n - 2} \end{array} \right| = D (n - 1, 0) = 1.$$
所以通过数学归纳法即得.
