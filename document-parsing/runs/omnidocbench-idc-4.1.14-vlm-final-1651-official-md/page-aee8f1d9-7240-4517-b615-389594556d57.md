# 例：求单位脉冲函数 $\delta(t)$ 的拉氏变换。

$$\delta (t) = \lim _ {t _ {0} \rightarrow 0} \frac{1 (t) - 1 \left(t - t _ {0}\right)}{t _ {0}}$$
  $$\begin{array}{l} L [ \delta (t) ] = \int_ {0} ^ {\infty} \delta (t) e ^ {- s t} d t = \int_ {0} ^ {\infty} \lim  _ {t _ {0} \rightarrow 0} \frac{1 (t) - 1 \left(t - t _ {0}\right)}{t _ {0}} e ^ {- s t} d t \\ = \lim _ {t _ {0} \rightarrow 0} \left[ \int_ {0} ^ {\infty} \frac{e ^ {- s t}}{t _ {0}} d t - \frac{1 (t - t _ {0}) e ^ {- s (t - t _ {0})}}{t _ {0}} e ^ {- s t _ {0}} d t \right] = \lim _ {t _ {0} \rightarrow 0} \frac{1 - e ^ {- t _ {0} s}}{t _ {0} s} = 1 \\ \end{array}$$
