  3. 积分定理

            $$F (s) = L [ f (t) ] \Rightarrow L [ \int_ {0} ^ {t} f (t) d t ] = \frac{F (s)}{s} + \frac{1}{s} f ^ {(- 1)} (0)$$
其中 $f^{(-1)}(0) = \int f(t) dt\Big|_{t=0}$
        $$L [ \underbrace{\int_ {0} ^ {t} \dots \int_ {0} ^ {t} f (t) d t \dots d t} _ {n} ] = \frac{F (s)}{s ^ {n}} + \frac{1}{s ^ {n}} f ^ {(- 1)} (0) + \dots + \frac{1}{s} f ^ {(- n)} (0)$$
      其中 $f^{(-n)}(0) = \underbrace{\int_0^t\ldots\int_0^t f(t)dt\ldots dt}_{n}\bigg|_{t = 0}^n$
4. 初值定理（重点掌握）

          $$\Rightarrow \lim  _ {t \rightarrow 0} f (t) = \lim  _ {s \rightarrow \infty} s F (s)$$
