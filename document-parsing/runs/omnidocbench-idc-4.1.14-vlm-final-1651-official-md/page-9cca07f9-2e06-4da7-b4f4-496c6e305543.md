## F.2. One-Step Recurrence for the Biased Convergence of the Finite-Particle Algorithm

### F.2.1. ONE-STEP RECURRENCE IN $W_2$ DISTANCE

Lemma 22 Assume Assumption 1. Let $\mathbf{z}_k \sim \rho_k^{\mathbf{z},\eta}$ for any $\rho_k^{\mathbf{z},\eta} \in \mathcal{P}(\mathbb{R}^{2dN})$, and let $\mathbf{z}_{k+1} \sim \rho_{k+1}^{\mathbf{z},\eta}$ be one step of the finite-particle algorithm (20) with step size $0 < \eta \leq \frac{\alpha}{64L^2}$. Then:
        $$W _ {2} \left(\rho_ {k + 1} ^ {\mathbf{z}, \eta}, \bar{\nu} ^ {\mathbf{Z}}\right) ^ {2} \leq e ^ {- \frac{3}{2} \alpha \eta} W _ {2} \left(\rho_ {k} ^ {\mathbf{z}, \eta}, \bar{\nu} ^ {\mathbf{Z}}\right) ^ {2} + \frac{8 \eta L ^ {2}}{\alpha} \left(\operatorname{V a r} _ {\bar{\nu} ^ {Z}} (\bar{Z}) + 6 4 \tau \eta d N\right).$$
Proof We consider a continuous-time interpolation of one step of the algorithm (20) as follows. Let $\mathbf{Z}_0 \sim \rho_0^{\mathbf{Z}}$ where we define $\rho_0^{\mathbf{Z}} = \rho_k^{\mathbf{z},\eta}$. We define $(\mathbf{Z}_t)_{0 \leq t \leq \eta}$ where $\mathbf{Z}_t \sim \rho_t^{\mathbf{Z}}$ evolves following:
            $$d \mathbf{Z} _ {t} = b ^ {\mathbf{Z}} \left(\mathbf{Z} _ {0}\right) d t + \sqrt{2 \tau} d W _ {t} ^ {\mathbf{Z}} \tag{49}$$
where $(W_{t}^{\mathbf{Z}})_{t\geq 0}$ is the standard Brownian motion in $\mathbb{R}^{2dN}$ which is independent of $\mathbf{Z}_0$. Then we notice that the distribution of $\mathbf{Z}_{\eta}\sim \rho_{\eta}^{\mathbf{Z}}$ of the process (49) is equal to the distribution of the next iterate $\mathbf{x}_{k + 1}\sim \rho_{k + 1}^{\mathbf{z},\eta}$ along the algorithm (20), i.e., $\rho_{k + 1}^{\mathbf{z},\eta} = \rho_{\eta}^{\mathbf{Z}}$. This is because the solution to the process (49) at time $t = \eta$ is:
          $$\mathbf{Z} _ {t} = \mathbf{Z} _ {0} + t b ^ {\mathbf{Z}} (\mathbf{Z} _ {0}) + \sqrt{2 \tau} W _ {t} ^ {\mathbf{Z}} \stackrel{d} {=} \mathbf{Z} _ {0} + t b ^ {\mathbf{Z}} (\mathbf{Z} _ {0}) + \sqrt{2 \tau t} \boldsymbol{\zeta}$$
where $\zeta \sim \mathcal{N}(0,I)$ is an independent Gaussian random variable in $\mathbb{R}^{2dN}$ , and $\stackrel{d}{=}$ means equality in distribution. This is the same update of the algorithm (20), so indeed $\mathbf{Z}_{\eta} \stackrel{d}{=} \mathbf{z}_{k+1}$ , so $\rho_{\eta}^{\mathbf{Z}} = \rho_{k+1}^{\mathbf{z},\eta}$ .

            $$d \bar{\mathbf{Z}} _ {t} = \bar{b} ^ {\mathbf{Z}} \left(\bar{\mathbf{Z}} _ {t}\right) d t + \sqrt{2 \tau} d W _ {t} ^ {\mathbf{Z}} \tag{50}$$
