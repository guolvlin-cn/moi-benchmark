# HOMOGENEOUS LINEAR SYSTEMS

  ■ Solve for the basic variables $x_{1}$ and $x_{2}$ to obtain $x_{1} = \frac{4}{3} x_{3}, x_{2} = 0$, with $x_{3}$ free.
    ■ As a vector, the general solution of Ax = 0 has the form given below.
      $$\mathbf{x} = \left[ \begin{array}{l} x _ {1} \\ x _ {2} \\ x _ {3} \end{array} \right] = \left[ \begin{array}{l} \frac{4}{3} x _ {3} \\ 0 \\ x _ {3} \end{array} \right] = x _ {3} \left[ \begin{array}{l} \frac{4}{3} \\ 0 \\ 1 \end{array} \right] = x _ {3} \mathbf{v}, \mathrm{where} \mathbf{v} = \left[ \begin{array}{l} \frac{4}{3} \\ 0 \\ 1 \end{array} \right]$$
