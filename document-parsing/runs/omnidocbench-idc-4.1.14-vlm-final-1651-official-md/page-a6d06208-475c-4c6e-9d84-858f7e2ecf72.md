Therefore, taking the expectation of both sides of the above equation over $\theta_{k+1}$ conditioned on $\mathcal{F}_k$, we have
      $$\begin{array}{l} \mathbb{E} \left[ \left| \left| C (X _ {k + 1}) - Y _ {k + 1} \right| \right| ^ {2} \right\rvert   \mathcal{F} _ {k} \bigg ] \\ = (1 - b _ {k}) ^ {2} \| C (X _ {k}) - Y _ {k} \| ^ {2} + \mathbb{E} \left[ \| b _ {k} I _ {1, k} - I _ {2, k} + I _ {3, k} \| ^ {2} \mid \mathcal{F} _ {k} \right] \\ + 2 \left(1 - b _ {k}\right) \langle C \left(X _ {k}\right) - Y _ {k}, \mathbb{E} \left[ b _ {k} I _ {1, k} - I _ {2, k} + I _ {3, k} \mid \mathcal{F} _ {k} \right] \rangle \\ \end{array}$$
  $$\begin{array}{l} \stackrel{(i)} {=} (1 - b _ {k}) ^ {2} \| C (X _ {k}) - Y _ {k} \| ^ {2} + b _ {k} ^ {2} \mathbb{E} \left[ \| I _ {1, k} \| ^ {2} \mid \mathcal{F} _ {k} \right] + \mathbb{E} \left[ \| I _ {2, k} \| ^ {2} \mid \mathcal{F} _ {k} \right] + \| I _ {3, k} \| ^ {2} - 2 b _ {k} \mathbb{E} \left[ \langle I _ {1, k}, I _ {2, k} \rangle \mid \mathcal{F} _ {k} \right] \\ + 2 b _ {k} \left\langle \mathbb{E} \left[ I _ {1, k} \mid \mathcal{F} _ {k} \right], I _ {3, k} \right\rangle - 2 \left\langle \mathbb{E} \left[ I _ {1, k} \mid \mathcal{F} _ {k} \right], I _ {3, k} \right\rangle \\ \end{array}$$
  $$\stackrel{(i i)} {=} (1 - b _ {k}) ^ {2} \| C (X _ {k}) - Y _ {k} \| ^ {2} + b _ {k} ^ {2} \mathbb{E} \left[ \| I _ {1, k} \| ^ {2} \mid \mathcal{F} _ {k} \right] + \mathbb{E} \left[ \| I _ {2, k} \| ^ {2} \mid \mathcal{F} _ {k} \right] - \| I _ {3, k} \| ^ {2} - 2 b _ {k} \mathbb{E} \left[ \langle I _ {1, k}, I _ {2, k} \rangle \mid \mathcal{F} _ {k} \right]$$
  $$\begin{array}{l} \stackrel{(\text{iii})} {\leq} \left(1 - b _ {k}\right) ^ {2} \| C (X _ {k}) - Y _ {k} \| ^ {2} + 2 b _ {k} ^ {2} \mathbb{E} \left[ \| I _ {1, k} \| ^ {2} \mid \mathcal{F} _ {k} \right] + 2 \mathbb{E} \left[ \| I _ {2, k} \| ^ {2} \mid \mathcal{F} _ {k} \right] \\ \leq \left(1 - b _ {k}\right) ^ {2} \| C (X _ {k}) - Y _ {k} \| ^ {2} + 2 b _ {k} ^ {2} \| X _ {k} \| ^ {2} \mathbb{E} \left[ \| M - M _ {\theta_ {k + 1}} \| ^ {2} \mid \mathcal{F} _ {k} \right] + 2 \mathbb{E} \left[ \| I _ {2, k} \| ^ {2} \mid \mathcal{F} _ {k} \right] \\ \end{array}$$
  $$\stackrel{(i v)} {\leq} (1 - b _ {k}) ^ {2} \| C (X _ {k}) - Y _ {k} \| ^ {2} + 2 b _ {k} ^ {2} \tau_ {x} ^ {4} v _ {m} ^ {2} + 4 \tau_ {x} ^ {2} \tau_ {m} ^ {2} \| X _ {k + 1} - X _ {k} \| ^ {2},$$
where the equations (i) and (ii) use the facts that $\mathbb{E}[I_{1,k}|\mathcal{F}_k] = 0$ and $\mathbb{E}[I_{2,k}|\mathcal{F}_k] = \mathbb{E}[I_{3,k}|\mathcal{F}_k] = 0$, the inequality (iii) follows from the Cauchy-Schwarz inequality $-2b_k\langle I_{2,k},I_{3,k}\rangle \leq b_k^2\| I_{1,k}\|^2 +\| I_{2,k}\|^2$, and the inequality (iv) leverages the result in (42). The proof is completed.
