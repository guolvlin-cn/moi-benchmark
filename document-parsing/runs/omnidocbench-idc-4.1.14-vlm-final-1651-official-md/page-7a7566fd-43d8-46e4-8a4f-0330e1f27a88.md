Proof. For any $z_1, z_2 \in \mathbb{C}$, let $z^{\theta} = (1 - \theta)z_1 + \theta z_2$ and let $\gamma(\theta) = f(|z^{\theta}|^2)z^{\theta}$ for $0 \leq \theta \leq 1$, we have
  $$f \left(\left| z _ {2} \right| ^ {2}\right) z _ {2} - f \left(\left| z _ {1} \right| ^ {2}\right) z _ {1} = \gamma (1) - \gamma (0) = \int_ {0} ^ {1} \gamma^ {\prime} (\theta) \mathrm{d} \theta , \tag{3.0.19}$$
where

$$\gamma^ {\prime} (\theta) = (1 + \sigma) f \left(\left| z ^ {\theta} \right| ^ {2}\right) \left(z _ {2} - z _ {1}\right) + g \left(z ^ {\theta}\right) \overline{{\left(z _ {2} - z _ {1}\right)}}, \tag{3.0.20}$$
with

          $$g (z) = \left\{ \begin{array}{l l} f ^ {\prime} \left(| z | ^ {2}\right) z ^ {2} = \beta \sigma | z | ^ {2 \sigma - 2} z ^ {2}, & z \neq 0, \\ 0, & z = 0, \end{array} \right. \quad z \in \mathbb{C}. \tag{3.0.21}$$
Then we have, since $\sigma > 0$ ,
      $$\left| f \left(\left| z _ {2} \right| ^ {2}\right) z _ {2} - f \left(\left| z _ {1} \right| ^ {2}\right) z _ {1} \right| \leq \int_ {0} ^ {1} \left| \gamma^ {\prime} (\theta) \right| \mathrm{d} \theta \lesssim \left| z _ {\theta} \right| ^ {2 \sigma} \left| z _ {1} - z _ {2} \right| \leq M _ {0} ^ {2 \sigma} \left| z _ {1} - z _ {2} \right|, \tag{3.0.22}$$
which completes the proof.

  We say $F:\mathbb{C}\to \mathbb{C}$ is (globally) $p$-Hölder continuous with $0 < p\leq 1$ if
        $$| F (z _ {1}) - F (z _ {2}) | \leq C | z _ {1} - z _ {2} | ^ {p}, \quad z _ {1}, z _ {2} \in \mathbb{C},$$
where $C$ is a constant independent of $z_{1}$ and $z_{2}$ .

## Lemma 3.2. Let $\frac{\alpha}{4} < \sigma \leq \frac{1}{2}$ and $F$ be $2\sigma$-Hölder continuous. For any $v \in H_{\mathrm{per}}^{1}(\Omega)$, we have $F(v) \in H_{\mathrm{per}}^{\frac{\alpha}{2}}(\Omega)$ and

            $$\left\| F (v) \right\| _ {H ^ {\frac{\alpha}{2}}} \lesssim \| v \| _ {H ^ {1}} ^ {2 \sigma}. \tag{3.0.23}$$
Proof. From Lemma 2.1 in (Zhang & Wang, 2024), recalling that $\Omega$ is equipped with the periodic boundary condition, we have
    $$\begin{array}{l} | F (v) | _ {H ^ {\frac{\alpha}{2}}} ^ {2} \lesssim \int_ {\Omega} \int_ {\Omega} \frac{| F (v (x + y)) - F (v (y)) | ^ {2}}{| x | ^ {1 + \alpha}} \text{dxdy} \\ \lesssim \int_ {\Omega} \int_ {\Omega} \frac{| v (x + y) - v (y) | ^ {4 \sigma}}{| x | ^ {1 + \alpha}} \text{dxdy} \quad (F \text{is} 2 \sigma - \text{Höldercontinuous}) \\ = \int_ {\Omega} \int_ {\Omega} \frac{\left| \int_ {0} ^ {1} v ^ {\prime} (y + \theta x) x d \theta \right| ^ {4 \sigma}}{| x | ^ {1 + \alpha}} \text{dxdy} \\ \leq \int_ {\Omega} \int_ {\Omega} \int_ {0} ^ {1} | v ^ {\prime} (y + \theta x) | ^ {4 \sigma} \mathrm{d} \theta \frac{| x | ^ {4 \sigma}}{| x | ^ {1 + \alpha}} \mathrm{d} x \mathrm{d} y \tag{3.0.24} \\ \lesssim \int_ {\Omega} \frac{| x | ^ {4 \sigma}}{| x | ^ {1 + \alpha}} \mathrm{d} x \int_ {\Omega} \int_ {0} ^ {1} | v ^ {\prime} (y) | ^ {4 \sigma} \mathrm{d} \theta \mathrm{d} y \quad (\text{changeintegrationorder}) \\ = \int_ {\Omega} \frac{| x | ^ {4 \sigma}}{| x | ^ {1 + \alpha}} d x \| v ^ {\prime} \| _ {L ^ {4 \sigma}} ^ {4 \sigma} \\ \lesssim \int_ {\Omega} \frac{| x | ^ {4 \sigma}}{| x | ^ {1 + \alpha}} d x \| v \| _ {H ^ {1}} ^ {4 \sigma} \quad (\sigma \leq \frac{1}{2}) \\ \lesssim \| v \| _ {H ^ {1}} ^ {4 \sigma} \quad (\sigma > \frac{\alpha}{4}, \text{theintegral} \int_ {\Omega} \frac{| x | ^ {4 \sigma}}{| x | ^ {1 + \alpha}} \mathrm{d} x \text{converges}). \\ \end{array}$$
Thus, we have completed the proof.
