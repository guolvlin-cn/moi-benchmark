For some $x_i \notin ((\mu^{-1}(\hat{Y}\tau^{-\varepsilon}))^{-1}, \mu^{-1}(\hat{Y}\tau^{-\varepsilon}))$, we may as well assume that there exist $i_1 \in \{m_1, \dots, m_{s_1}\}$ and $i_2 \in \{n_1, \dots, n_{s_2}\}$ such that $x_{i_1} > \mu^{-1}(\hat{Y}\tau^{-\varepsilon})$ and $x_{i_2} < (\mu^{-1}(\hat{Y}\tau^{-\varepsilon}))^{-1}$, respectively. Let $s_1 := \{m_1, \dots, m_{s_1}\}$ and $s_2 := \{n_1, \dots, n_{s_2}\}$. Then
  $$\langle D f _ {\tau} (x) y, y \rangle + m _ {0} | D g _ {\tau} (x) y | ^ {2} = \sum_ {i = 1, i \notin s _ {1} \cup s _ {2}} ^ {d} \left(\frac{\partial f (x)}{\partial x _ {i}} y _ {i} ^ {2} + m _ {0} \Big | \sum_ {j = 1} ^ {m} \frac{\partial g ^ {j} (x)}{\partial x _ {i}} y _ {i} \Big | ^ {2}\right) + M _ {1} + M _ {2}, \tag{D2}$$
where

  $$M _ {1} = \sum_ {i = m _ {1}} ^ {m _ {s _ {1}}} \left( \right.\frac{\partial f _ {\tau} (x)}{\partial x _ {i}} y _ {i} ^ {2} + m _ {0} \left. \right\lvert \sum_ {j = 1} ^ {m} \left. \frac{\partial g _ {\tau} ^ {j} (x)}{\partial x _ {i}} y _ {i} \right| ^ {2}\left. \right)$$
and

  $$M _ {2} = \sum_ {i = n _ {1}} ^ {n _ {s _ {2}}} \left(\frac{\partial f _ {\tau} (x)}{\partial x _ {i}} y _ {i} ^ {2} + m _ {0} \Big | \sum_ {j = 1} ^ {m} \frac{\partial g _ {\tau} ^ {j} (x)}{\partial x _ {i}} y _ {i} \Big | ^ {2}\right).$$
We obtain the following estimation of $M_{1}$ from (D1) that

    $$\begin{array}{l} M _ {1} = \sum_ {i = m _ {1}} ^ {m _ {s _ {1}}} \left(\frac{\partial f (\Phi (x _ {1}) , \cdots , \Phi (x _ {d}))}{\partial \Phi (x _ {i})} y _ {i} ^ {2} \cdot \frac{1}{1 + (x _ {i} - \mu^ {- 1} (\hat{Y} \tau^ {- \varepsilon})) ^ {\bar{\nu}}} \right. \\ + m _ {0} \Big | \sum_ {j = 1} ^ {m} \frac{\partial g ^ {j} (\Phi (x _ {1}) , \cdots , \Phi (x _ {d}))}{\partial \Phi (x _ {i})} y _ {i} \cdot \frac{1}{1 + (x _ {i} - \mu^ {- 1} (\hat{Y} \tau^ {- \varepsilon})) ^ {\bar{\nu}}} \Big | ^ {2} \Big) \\ = \sum_ {i = m _ {1}} ^ {m _ {s _ {1}}} \left(\frac{1}{(1 + (x _ {i} - \mu^ {- 1} (\hat{Y} \tau^ {- \varepsilon})) ^ {\bar{\nu}}) ^ {2}} \cdot \left[ \frac{\partial f (\Phi (x _ {1}) , \cdots , \Phi (x _ {d}))}{\partial \Phi (x _ {i})} y _ {i} ^ {2} \right. \right. \\ + m _ {0} \Big | \sum_ {j = 1} ^ {m} \frac{\partial g ^ {j} (\Phi (x _ {1}) , \cdots , \Phi (x _ {d}))}{\partial \Phi (x _ {i})} y _ {i} \Big | ^ {2} \Big ] + \frac{(x _ {i} - \mu^ {- 1} (\hat{Y} \tau^ {- \varepsilon})) ^ {\bar{\nu}}}{(1 + (x _ {i} - \mu^ {- 1} (\hat{Y} \tau^ {- \varepsilon})) ^ {\bar{\nu}}) ^ {2}} \frac{\partial f (\Phi (x _ {1}) , \cdots , \Phi (x _ {d}))}{\partial \Phi (x _ {i})} y _ {i} ^ {2} \Big) \\ \leq \sum_ {i = m _ {1}} ^ {m _ {s _ {1}}} \left[ \frac{\partial f (\Phi (x _ {1}) , \cdots , \Phi (x _ {d}))}{\partial \Phi (x _ {i})} y _ {i} ^ {2} + m _ {0} \right| \sum_ {j = 1} ^ {m} \frac{\partial g _ {j} (\Phi (x _ {1}) , \cdots , \Phi (x _ {d}))}{\partial \Phi (x _ {i})} y _ {i} \Big | ^ {2} \Big ] \\ \leq C \sum_ {i = m _ {1}} ^ {m _ {s _ {1}}} y _ {i} ^ {2}. \tag{D3} \\ \end{array}$$
For $M_2$, we analogously obtain
  $$M _ {2} \leq \sum_ {i = n _ {1}} ^ {n _ {s _ {2}}} \left[ \frac{\partial f (\Phi (x _ {1}) , \cdots , \Phi (x _ {d}))}{\partial \Phi (x _ {i})} y _ {i} ^ {2} + m _ {0} \right| \sum_ {j = 1} ^ {m} \frac{\partial g ^ {j} (\Phi (x _ {1}) , \cdots , \Phi (x _ {d}))}{\partial \Phi (x _ {i})} y _ {i} \Big | ^ {2} ] \leq C \sum_ {i = n _ {1}} ^ {n _ {s _ {2}}} y _ {i} ^ {2}. \tag{D4}$$
Substituting (D3) and (D4) into (D2) yields that for sufficiently large $m_0 > 0$ and any $x,y\in \mathbb{R}^d$
  $$\langle D f _ {\tau} (x) y, y \rangle + m _ {0} | D g _ {\tau} (x) y | ^ {2} \leq C | y | ^ {2}. \tag{D5}$$
