1. Simplify $(\alpha \beta^{3})(\alpha^{-2}\beta^{4})^{5}$ and express your answer with positive indices. (3 marks)
  $$\begin{array}{l} (a _ {3} ^ {3}) (a ^ {- 2} b ^ {4}) ^ {5} \\ = (\alpha \beta^ {3}) (\alpha^ {- 1 0} \beta^ {2 0}) \\ = \alpha (\beta^ {3}) (\alpha^ {- 1 0}) (\beta^ {2 0}) \\ = a (\alpha^ {- 1 0}) (\beta^ {3}) (\beta^ {2 0}) \\ = \alpha \beta \\ \end{array}$$
2. Make $a$ the subject of the formula $\frac{4 - 3a}{b} = 5$. (3 marks)
    $$\begin{array}{l} \frac{4 - 3 a}{b} = 5 \\ 4 - 3 a = 5 b \\ 3 a = 4 - 5 b   a = \frac{4 - 5 b}{3} \\ \end{array}$$
