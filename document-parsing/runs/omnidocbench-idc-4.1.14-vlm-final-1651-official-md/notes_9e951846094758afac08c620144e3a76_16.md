        7. 如图，梯形 $ABCD$ 中：$AB // CD$. 若 $AB = 1$,
          $CD=2$, $AC=6$, $S_{\Delta AOB}=1$. 则 $AO=$ _____.

          $S_{梯形ABCD}=$

    解： $\because AB // CD$
              $$\therefore \triangle \text{AOB} \sim \Delta \text{COD}$$
            即 $\frac{AO}{CO} = \frac{AB}{CD}$，$\because AB=1, CD=2, AC=6$
        $$\therefore \frac{A O}{C O} = \frac{1}{2} \Rightarrow C O = 2 A O$$
        又 \because AC = AO + CO = 3AO \Rightarrow 3AO = 6, AO = 2
    又 \because $\frac{{S}_{\Delta AOB}}{{S}_{\Delta COD}} = {\left( \frac{AB}{CD}\right) }^{2} = {\left( \frac{1}{2}\right) }^{2} = \frac{1}{4} = \frac{1}{{S}_{\Delta COD}}$
    $\therefore S_{\Delta COD} = 4$
    \because S_{\Delta AOB} 与 S_{\Delta BOC} (不同底，同高)
      且底之比为 $OA : OC = 1 : 2$

    $\therefore S_{\Delta AOB} : S_{\Delta BOC} = 1 : 2$ $\because S_{\Delta AOB} = 1$
    \therefore S_{\Delta BOC} = 2. 同理：S_{\Delta AOD} 与 S_{\Delta COD} (不同底,同高)
  且底之比为 $OA:OC=1:2$，$\therefore S_{\triangle AOD}:S_{\triangle COD}=1:2$
