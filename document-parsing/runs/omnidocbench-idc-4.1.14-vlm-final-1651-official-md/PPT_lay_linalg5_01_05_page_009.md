#     SOLUTIONS OF NONHOMOGENEOUS SYSTEMS

■ Solution: Row operations on $[A \quad 0]$ produce
  $$\left[ \begin{array}{\text{rrr} r} 3 & 5 & - 4 & 7 \\ - 3 & - 2 & 4 & - 1 \\ 6 & 1 & - 8 & - 4 \end{array} \right] \sim \left[ \begin{array}{\text{rrr} r} 1 & 0 & - \frac{4}{3} & - 1 \\ 0 & 1 & 0 & 2 \\ 0 & 0 & 0 & 0 \end{array} \right], \quad x _ {1} - \frac{4}{3} x _ {3} = - 1 \\ x _ {2} = 2 \\ 0 = 0$$
      ■ Thus $x_{1} = -1 + \frac{4}{3} x_{3}$, $x_{2} = 2$, and $x_{3}$ is free.
