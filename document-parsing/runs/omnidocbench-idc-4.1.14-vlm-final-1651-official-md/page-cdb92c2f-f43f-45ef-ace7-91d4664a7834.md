Let us consider that $(\mathcal{L},[\cdot_{\lambda}\cdot ],\alpha)$ is a Hom-Leibniz conformal algebra, which has coefficients in the representation $(l,r,\mathcal{M},\beta)$. The group of $n$th-cochain $C_{HomL}^n (\mathcal{L},M)$ contains the map $f_{\lambda_1,\dots ,\lambda_{n - 1}}:\wedge^{\otimes n}\mathcal{L}\to \mathbb{C}[\lambda_1,\dots ,\lambda_{n - 1}]\otimes \mathcal{M}$ defined by $p_1\otimes p_2\otimes \dots \otimes p_n\mapsto f_{\lambda_1,\lambda_2,\dots ,\lambda_{n - 1}}(p_1,p_2,\dots ,p_n)$. These maps satisfy the following conformal sesquilinearity condition:
  $$\begin{array}{l} f _ {\lambda_ {1}, \lambda_ {2}, \dots , \lambda_ {n - 1}} (p _ {1}, p _ {2}, \dots , \partial (p _ {i}), \dots , p _ {n}) \\ = \left\{ \begin{array}{l l} - \lambda_ {i} f _ {\lambda_ {1}, \lambda_ {2}, \dots , \lambda_ {n - 1}} \left(p _ {1}, p _ {2}, \dots , p _ {n}\right), & i = 1, \dots , n - 1, \\ \left(\partial + \lambda_ {1} + \lambda_ {2} + \dots + \lambda_ {n - 1}\right) f _ {\lambda_ {1}, \lambda_ {2}, \dots , \lambda_ {n - 1}} \left(p _ {1}, p _ {2}, \dots , p _ {n}\right), & i = n. \end{array} \right. \tag{12} \\ \end{array}$$
such that $f \circ \alpha^{\otimes n} = \beta \circ f$. The coboundary map $\delta_{Hom}: C_{Hom}^{n}(\mathcal{L},\mathcal{M}) \to C_{Hom}^{n+1}(\mathcal{L},\mathcal{M})$ can be defined by
    $$\begin{array}{l} \left(\delta_ {\text{Hom} L} f\right) _ {\lambda_ {1}, \dots , \lambda_ {n}} \left(p _ {1}, \dots , p _ {n + 1}\right) \\ = \sum_ {i = 1} ^ {n} (- 1) ^ {i + 1} l \left(\alpha^ {n - 1} \left(p _ {i}\right)\right) _ {\lambda_ {i}} f _ {\lambda_ {1}, \dots , \hat{\lambda} _ {i}, \dots , \lambda_ {n}} \left(p _ {1}, \dots , \hat{p} _ {i}, \dots , p _ {n + 1}\right) \tag{13} \\ + (- 1) ^ {n + 1} r \left(f _ {\lambda_ {1}, \dots , \lambda_ {n - 1}} \left(p _ {1}, \dots , p _ {n}\right)\right) _ {\lambda_ {1} + \dots + \lambda_ {n}} \alpha^ {n - 1} \left(p _ {n + 1}\right) \\ + \sum_ {i <   j} (- 1) ^ {i + j} f _ {\lambda_ {i} + \lambda_ {j}, \lambda_ {1}, \dots , \hat{\lambda_ {i}}, \dots , \hat{\lambda_ {j}}, \dots , \lambda_ {n}} ([ p _ {i \lambda_ {i}} p _ {j} ], \alpha (p _ {1}), \dots , \alpha (\hat{p} _ {i}), \dots , \alpha (\hat{p} _ {j}), \dots , \alpha (p _ {n + 1})), \\ \end{array}$$
where $f\in C_{HomL}^{n}(\mathcal{L},\mathcal{M})$ and $p_1,p_2,\dots ,p_n,p_{n + 1}\in \mathcal{L}$ . With coefficients in the representation $(l,r,\mathcal{M},\beta)$ , the corresponding cohomology groups are known as the cohomology of the Hom-Leibniz conformal algebra $(\mathcal{L},[\cdot_{\lambda}\cdot ],\alpha)$ . If we assume that $l(p)_{\lambda}m = [p_{\lambda}m]$ and $r(m)_{\lambda}p = [m_{\lambda}p]$ then above equations turn to be
    $$\begin{array}{l} \left(\delta_ {\text{Hom} L} f\right) _ {\lambda_ {1}, \dots , \lambda_ {n}} \left(p _ {1}, \dots , p _ {n + 1}\right) \\ = \sum_ {i = 1} ^ {n} (- 1) ^ {i + 1} \left[ \alpha^ {n - 1} \left(p _ {i}\right) _ {\lambda_ {i}} f _ {\lambda_ {1}, \dots , \hat{\lambda_ {i}}, \dots , \lambda_ {n}} \left(p _ {1}, \dots , \hat{p _ {i}}, \dots , p _ {n + 1}\right) \right] \tag{14} \\ + (- 1) ^ {n + 1} \left[ f _ {\lambda_ {1}, \dots , \lambda_ {n - 1}} \left(p _ {1}, \dots , p _ {n}\right) _ {\lambda_ {1} + \dots + \lambda_ {n}} \alpha^ {n - 1} \left(p _ {n + 1}\right) \right] \\ + \sum_ {i <   j} (- 1) ^ {i + j} f _ {\lambda_ {i} + \lambda_ {j}, \lambda_ {1}, \dots , \hat{\lambda_ {i}}, \dots , \hat{\lambda_ {j}}, \dots , \lambda_ {n}} ([ p  p _ {j} ], \alpha (p _ {1}), \dots , \alpha (\hat{p _ {i}}), \dots , \alpha (\hat{p _ {j}}), \dots , \alpha (p _ {n + 1})), \\ \end{array}$$
