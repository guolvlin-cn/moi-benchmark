  Assume that $\mathcal{M}$ halts. Let $N$ be the number of time steps it takes $\mathcal{M}$ to halt. For every $n \geq N$, by Corollary 2.60, $\nu_{n}^{\mathcal{M},\lambda}$ has a perfect ZPC strategy. So, if $C \geq N$, then we are done. Otherwise, let $n$ be such that
            $$\max  (C, \log N) \leq n <   N.$$
By Lemma 2.59, $\mathcal{V}_n^{\mathcal{M},\lambda} = (\mathcal{V}^{\mathcal{M},\lambda})_n'$ in this case. By the compression theorem 2.53, since $n \geq C$, $(\mathcal{V}^{\mathcal{M},\lambda})_n'$ has a perfect ZPC strategy given that $\mathcal{V}_{2^n}^{\mathcal{M},\lambda}$ has one. But $2^n \geq N$, and we already argued that these tailored games have perfect ZPC strategies. Hence, $\mathcal{V}_n^{\mathcal{M},\lambda}$ has a perfect ZPC strategy when $n \geq \max(C, \log N)$. If $C \geq \log N$, then we are done. Otherwise, we can iterate this argument and deduce the same for any $n \geq \max(C, \log \log N)$. Since there exists some $t$ for which $C \geq \underbrace{\log \dots \log N}_{t - \text{times}}$, we deduce that $\mathfrak{G}_{\mathcal{M}} = \mathcal{V}_C^{\mathcal{M},\lambda}$ has a perfect ZPC strategy. This proves (2) in Theorem 2.31.
  Assume that $\mathcal{M}$ does not halt. Then, by Lemma 2.59,
              $$\mathcal{V} _ {n} ^ {\mathcal{M}, \lambda} = \left(\mathcal{V} ^ {\mathcal{M}, \lambda}\right) _ {n} ^ {\prime} \tag{11}$$
for every $n$. If $n \geq C$, then by the compression theorem 2.53, we have
    $$\mathcal{E} \left(\left(\mathcal{V} ^ {\mathcal{M}, \lambda}\right) _ {n} ^ {\prime}, 1 / 2\right) \geq \mathcal{E} \left(\mathcal{V} _ {2 ^ {n}} ^ {\mathcal{M}, \lambda}, 1 / 2\right) \quad \text{and} \quad \mathcal{E} \left(\left(\mathcal{V} ^ {\mathcal{M}, \lambda}\right) _ {n} ^ {\prime}, 1 / 2\right) \geq \underbrace{2 ^ {2 ^ {\lambda n} - 1}} _ {\geq 2 ^ {n}}. \tag{12}$$
So, for every positive integer t we can deduce that

          $$\mathcal{E} (\mathcal{V} _ {C} ^ {\mathcal{M}, \lambda}, ^ {1 / 2}) = _ {(1 1)} \mathcal{E} ((\mathcal{V} ^ {\mathcal{M}, \lambda}) _ {C} ^ {\prime}, ^ {1 / 2})$$
        $$\geq_ {(1 2)} \mathcal{E} \big (\mathcal{V} _ {2 ^ {c}} ^ {\mathcal{M}, \lambda}, ^ {1 / 2} \big) = _ {(1 1)} \mathcal{E} \big ((\mathcal{V} ^ {\mathcal{M}, \lambda}) _ {2 ^ {c}} ^ {\prime}, ^ {1 / 2} \big)$$

      $$\geq_ {(1 2)} \mathscr{E} \left(\mathcal{V} _ {2 ^ {C}} ^ {\mathcal{M}, \lambda}, 1 / 2\right) = _ {(1 1)} \mathscr{E} \left(\left(\mathcal{V} _ {2 ^ {C}} ^ {\mathcal{M}, \lambda}\right) ^ {\prime} \quad_ {2 ^ {C}}, 1 / 2\right)$$
            $$\begin{array}{c c} \underbrace{2 \ddots} _ {t - \text{time} s} & \underbrace{2 \ddots} _ {t - \text{time} s} \end{array}$$
                $$\geq_ {(1 2)} \underbrace{2 ^ {\cdot^ {\cdot^ {2 ^ {C}}}}} _ {(t + 1) - \text{time} s}.$$
Since this was true for every $t$, we can deduce that $\mathcal{E}(\mathcal{V}_C^{\mathcal{M},\lambda},1 / 2) = \infty$, which in turn proves that
          $$\operatorname{v a l} ^ {*} \left(\mathfrak{G} _ {\mathcal{M}}\right) = \operatorname{v a l} ^ {*} \left(\mathcal{V} _ {C} ^ {\mathcal{M}, \lambda}\right) <   1 / 2,$$
proving (3) in Theorem 2.31.

  The rest of the paper is devoted to the proof of Compression, Theorem 2.53.
