# Matrix representation of system of linear equations

      Given a system of linear equations $\left\{ \begin{array}{l} x_{1} - 2x_{2} + x_{3} = 0 \\ 2x_{2} - 8x_{3} = 8 \\ -4x_{1} + 5x_{2} + 9x_{3} = -9 \end{array} \right.$
  The coefficient matrix is $\left[ \begin{array}{rrr}1 & -2 & 1\\ 0 & 2 & -8\\ -4 & 5 & 9 \end{array} \right]$
    The augmented matrix is $\left[ \begin{array}{rrrr}1 & -2 & 1 & 0\\ 0 & 2 & -8 & 8\\ -4 & 5 & 9 & -9 \end{array} \right]$
