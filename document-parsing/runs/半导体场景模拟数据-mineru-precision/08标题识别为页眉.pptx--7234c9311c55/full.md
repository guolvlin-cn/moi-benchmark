**SEASF06B LDD Stage WAT VBG Fail 8\_D Report**

**D2: Problem Description**

Here are 5 key points about semiconductors in English:

1. \*\*Semiconductors\*\* are materials with electrical conductivity between conductors and insulators, enabling precise control of electron flow.

2. \*\*Silicon\*\* dominates the industry due to its abundance, ideal bandgap, and excellent native oxide properties for manufacturing.

3. \*\*Doping\*\* introduces impurities into pure semiconductors to create N-type (electron-rich) or P-type (hole-rich) materials essential for device operation.

4. \*\*CMOS technology\*\* combines complementary MOSFETs to achieve low power consumption and high noise immunity in modern integrated circuits.

5. \*\*Advanced nodes\*\* below 3nm now employ \*\*Gate-All-Around (GAA)\*\* transistors to overcome physical limitations and maintain Moore's Law progression.
